DogecoinVM is a Dogecoin-compatible chain. It uses the same kind of address, and the coins are separate. dogecoin:D...?amount=1.5 pays Dogecoin, usually in 1 to 10 minutes. dogecoinvm:D...?amount=1.5 pays DogecoinVM, final in about a fifth of a second. metaldoge.com explains what it is and how to use it.
