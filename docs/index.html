A self-custodial Dogecoin wallet for IRC, social platforms, and the open web

Before you begin

Your wallet. Your keys. Your responsibility.

DogeDrop is a self-custodial wallet. Your private keys and recovery phrase belong exclusively to you. They are generated and stored locally in your browser, never on our servers.

key Truly self-custodial

We cannot access your wallet, view your recovery phrase, recover your password, or authorize transactions on your behalf. Your keys remain under your control.

backup Back up your recovery phrase

Your 12-word recovery phrase is the only reliable way to restore your wallet. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe, preferably offline. Never share it with anyone or enter it on an untrusted website.

If you lose your recovery phrase and access to your wallet, nobody can recover your funds. Not even us.

devices Browser storage is not permanent

Your encrypted wallet is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing website data, using private browsing, changing devices, or browser storage cleanup may permanently remove the local copy.

Your Dogecoin remains on the blockchain, but without your recovery phrase you may lose access to it.

science MetalDoge is experimental

DogeDrop supports Dogecoin and DogecoinVM, part of the emerging MetalDoge ecosystem. DogecoinVM technology is still experimental and should be considered alpha software.

Features, infrastructure, and network behavior may change. Use small amounts and understand the risks before experimenting.

code Open technology

This wallet is based on the open-source DogeDrop, originally developed by Paulo Vidal, and further developed by SimosNap IRC Network.

Our goal is to bring self-custodial Dogecoin payments to the SimosNap IRC Network and Monoverse, our framework for extending IRC channels with social features and web integration. The wallet remains open source and available for anyone who wants to integrate it into their own website or application.

The wallet has not been independently audited. Always exercise caution when using experimental financial software.
